# v1.4 expansion review

12 October 2026 · 68 → 84 species · 136 → 168 SVGs

The [full existing-set audit](AUDIT-1.4.md) preceded the expansion. Two groups of eight were researched and drawn sequentially. Round one passed source comparison, enlarged and intended-size inspection, and the complete geometry check before round two was drawn. New paths are original constructions; source illustrations and text are not traced or redistributed.

## Round one: oaks and shrubs

The selection fills gaps in the oaks and familiar native or planted shrubs. Downy oak, Silver lime and Red elder intentionally retain resemblance to relatives: omitted hair, underside, pith or reproductive characters cannot be replaced with invented silhouettes.

Review corrections before round two: shortened two branches in the new oak drawings, separated the upper Red elder leaflets from the terminal leaflet, shortened one Sea buckthorn vein and reduced its Compact midrib weight. The round-one checkpoint passed 152 SVGs and 1417 internal vein/midrib paths, including full stroke footprints and separate leaflet outlines.

## Round two: conifers and park foliage

The second group adds meaningful structural contrasts: five- versus two-needle fascicles, opposite flat needles, basal-toothed versus fully serrate compound leaflets, and deep- versus shallow-lobed maples. The set now contains nine Acer species and ten conifers. London plane was already included in v1.3 and remains unchanged.

The [University of Washington Burke Herbarium / FNA account](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Acer+saccharinum) specifically supports Silver maple's deep sinuses rounded at their bases. The [North Carolina State University Acer key](https://herbarium.ncsu.edu/tnc/acer.htm) contrasts its narrow-based central lobe with Red maple's broader-based, shallower lobes. [Flora Novae Angliae via Go Botany](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/acer/saccharinum/) supports concentrating Silver maple teeth toward the outer lobe portions. Dawn redwood's opposite linear, flat, blunt needles follow the linked university account; the icon shows one deciduous branchlet, not an entire shoot system.

The [Virginia Tech dendrology account for Swiss stone pine](https://dendro.cnre.vt.edu/dendrology/syllabus/factsheet.cfm?ID=663) also describes the deciduous fascicle sheath, supporting the selected mature, sheath-free bundles.

Review corrections: increased clearance below the Tree-of-heaven terminal leaflet; routed the Silver maple primary vein through the actual connected blade between its concave sinuses. Both centerline and full-stroke containment were rechecked, without clipping or changing path roles to hide failures.

## Final review

Both rounds were inspected enlarged and at 24/32 px Compact and 48/64 px Detail on light and dark backgrounds. Comparison groups included all five oaks, adjacent maples, the three pines, both elders, raspberry and other compound leaves, the three limes and narrow foliage. Compact retains diagnostic bundle membership but reduces needle-pair numbers on Dawn redwood and secondary venation elsewhere. Pair numbers and leaflet counts are selected examples; they are not universal species constants.

All 168 source SVGs pass geometry checks, including 1611 internal paths. Seven semantic artwork tests protect selected structures alongside twelve geometry regression tests. Both complete browser workflows, source-to-sprite comparisons and the archive check are recorded in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md). These establish technical properties and documented morphological choices, not external scientific certification.
