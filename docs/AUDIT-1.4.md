# Existing-set audit for v1.4.0

12 October 2026 · All 68 species present in v1.3.0 · Both optical sizes

## Scope and outcome

Every existing SVG was inspected on enlarged contact sheets (220 px per drawing), then Compact at 24/32 px and Detail at 48/64 px on light and dark backgrounds. Botanical descriptions were reopened for all 68 species. The review considered contour, apex, base, relative stalk length, lobes, teeth, venation, leaflet/bundle structure and the documented selected form. The final automated and browser runs also cover every existing and new SVG.

No additional source-supported shape correction was established in this review. All 136 existing source SVGs are byte-identical to the verified v1.3.0 archive, including Stieleiche, Bergulme and the eight v1.1 redraws. The [SHA-256 record](audit-1.4.json) records these preserved bytes. This finding means the selected stylizations remain consistent with their stated scope; it does not establish perfect specimens, independent botanical certification or reliable species identification from the icons alone.

Documentation corrections: the Drawing checks paragraph in BOTANY still contained the old 104-file/912-path totals; it now reports the current release. Ginkgo and Japanese maple references now point to the directly accessed FNA treatment and Flora of China PDF. Earlier release history remains intact.

## Additional checks and limits

The elm comparison also uses the existing measurements and the [Fragnière, Küffer & Kozlowski identification treatment](https://www.infoflora.ch/assets/content/documents/publication/Y_Fragniere_2022_Bestimmung_europaeischen_Ulmen.pdf). Wych elm is not redrawn from memory. The common juniper audit used the [nominate subspecies treatment](https://www.infoflora.ch/de/flora/juniperus-communis-subsp-communis.html) where the parent taxon page subdivides the species. Japanese maple is species 20, PDF pages 11–12, in the linked Flora of China treatment.

Surface hairs, wax, colours, texture, three-dimensional petiole/needle sections and seasonal changes are generally unencoded. Isolated leaves cannot demonstrate alternate versus opposite insertion on a shoot. Close relatives such as limes and elders may remain similar; forcing artificial differences would reduce scientific fidelity. Shape proportions represent selected typical forms rather than traced, scale-measured specimens. The review is a documented design assessment, not an independent specialist determination. See [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md) for measured technical checks and browser coverage.
