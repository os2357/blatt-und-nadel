# Full collection review — 1.5.0

12 October 2026 · **100 species / 200 source SVGs** · baseline: canonical v1.4.0 package

Both Detail and Compact were inspected enlarged, compared with botanical descriptions and reference photographs, and viewed at their intended display sizes on light and dark backgrounds. The table covers every species. Sources establish morphology; the accepted result is the explicitly reduced drawing, not every possible leaf of that taxon.

## What changed in the review

The previous Ginkgo check confirmed a fan, forked nerves and the absence of a midrib but missed the unnatural overall form and schematic nerve trajectories. It therefore offered too much reassurance. The current review separates whole-leaf visual similarity from feature counts and from SVG validity. It also checks base, apex, maximum-width position, petiole relationship, vein direction and near-neighbor species. See the [required gates](QUALITY.md#required-review-gates).

Foliage photographs from InfoFlora and Oregon State University were placed beside both icon variants. Additional photographs were consulted for Ginkgo, Mahonia and the two Ribes additions. Flower, fruit and habit photographs supply context but do not substitute for a visible blade or needle arrangement. Copyrighted reference photographs are not included in this repository; exact reference image URLs are recorded in [audit-1.5.json](audit-1.5.json).

The JSON audit records the baseline and current SHA-256 for each file.

## Measurements and technical evidence

The JSON record contains per-variant source hashes, path-role counts and per-blade bounding boxes. Blade bounds exclude the stalk and stroke width; compound leaves are measured per leaflet. These dimensions are useful for detecting regressions, not for claiming a physical specimen measurement or a universal natural ratio.

All 200 SVGs pass the XML/metadata, viewport, contour, blade separation, conifer connectivity and internal-stroke checks documented in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md). All 1951 internal paths pass the defined geometric and raster tolerances. Semantic tests protect selected biological structures, including forked Ginkgo veins, pine fascicle membership and opposite versus alternate conifer insertion. Technical tests cannot independently establish botanical accuracy.

## Remaining limits

Every icon selects one leaf or shoot form. The lower surface, hairs, stomatal bands, texture and most three-dimensional characters remain absent. Fine nerves and teeth are reduced, particularly in Compact. Several species therefore remain visually similar, and this is preferable to fabricated differences. Scientific use beyond an accompanying icon requires the species description and specimen evidence; independent botanical specialist review has not yet taken place.
