# Collection scope

Blatt & Nadel is a reusable icon collection for nature websites, species portraits and filters. Version 2.3.0 contains **200 woody taxa**, including ten maples, nine oaks and twenty-four conifers, in two optical sizes. It covers common woodland, hedgerow, riparian, garden and urban foliage, with selected alpine shrubs, planted trees and Mediterranean evergreens.

## When is the collection complete?

The 200-species collection is a useful core that can now be maintained without continual expansion. It is **not a complete flora of Central Europe**. Its boundary is practical use on nature websites, not every woody species, hybrid or horticultural cultivar.

A core release is ready when its chosen species list is covered, known drawing defects are resolved, every variant has a recorded source and visual review, technical and browser checks pass, and omissions are documented. Adding more species does not compensate for a weak existing drawing.

Before describing this collection as suitable for scientific identification, it needs an independent botanical review against identified specimens, with particular attention to variable taxa and features omitted from the icons. That review has not taken place. These icons are intended to accompany names and species accounts, not replace them.

The v2.3 artwork and geometry gates are complete for the reviewed gymnosperms and the 25 additions. The other 153 taxa were last reviewed in v2.2 and are scheduled for later review batches. The browser gate passed in Chromium; Firefox was not run in this environment. Both points are disclosed in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md), not recorded as passed checks.

## What the icons represent

- A selected mature leaf or defined shoot form. Juvenile, long-shoot, shade and cultivar forms can differ.
- A separately scaled drawing, not a scale comparison between species.
- A frontal monochrome view. Hairs, gland colour, surface texture, needle cross-sections and most lower-surface characters cannot be inferred from it.
- Distinct Detail and Compact drawings. Fine venation and tooth density are deliberately reduced at small sizes; counts stated in the documentation describe the drawing unless explicitly attributed to a flora.

Natural similarity is retained. The lime species, birches, willows and several compound leaves cannot always be reliably identified from these silhouettes. Do not invent a visible difference merely to make a filter icon unique.

## Further expansion

Prefer a separate, justified module over an open-ended species count. The 2.2 release added eleven broadleaf shrubs/trees/subshrubs and six conifers; 2.3 adds 25 taxa in three reviewed groups, including Mediterranean shrubs, bog and heath shrubs and three planted conifers ([EXPANSION-2.3.md](EXPANSION-2.3.md)). Lavendel and Rosmarin are included because they are woody; herbaceous plants are outside this scope. Further candidates should follow actual site gaps: well-defined regional willow taxa, specific evergreen garden trees or requested mycorrhizal hosts. Each proposal should identify a real content gap and a source-backed leaf form before drawing starts.

The next maintenance priority is completing the batch re-review of the 153 flowering-plant taxa not yet covered by [AUDIT-2.3.md](AUDIT-2.3.md), then feedback from real use and independent botanical review. The [quality procedure](QUALITY.md) and the per-species audits define the evidence required for corrections.
