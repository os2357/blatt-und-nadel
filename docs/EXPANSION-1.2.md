# Expansion review · v1.2.0

12 October 2026 · 24 added species · 48 new SVGs · 52 species / 104 SVGs in total

## Two review rounds

The first round added 16 trees and shrubs to extend the collection beyond its original forest-tree focus: Black elder, Common hawthorn, Blackthorn, Common dogwood, Guelder rose, European holly, Alder buckthorn, European spindle, Dog rose, Cornelian cherry, Wild cherry, Large-leaved lime, Common walnut, Downy birch, European white elm and Wild service tree.

After that round passed the outline and stroke checks and was inspected at its target sizes, the second round added eight species: Montpellier maple, Box elder, Wild privet, Common barberry, Fly honeysuckle, Common buckthorn, Snowy mespilus and Bird cherry. These expand the maple forms and the coverage of hedges, woodland margins and shrubs. The selection includes native and introduced species; it is not a planting recommendation.

Each new species has its own linked Info Flora description and a row in [BOTANY.md](BOTANY.md) recording the drawing choice and the omissions of each optical size. Both rounds used the same criteria: blade proportions, base and apex, petiole relationship, margin and lobes, primary venation, leaflet number and attachment, and features that can survive reduction to a small icon. Drawings select a plausible form within natural variation; they are not measurements of a voucher specimen.

## Features checked against additional references

- **European white elm:** a single apex, greatest width near the middle, pronounced basal asymmetry, short petiole and unbranched primary veins. Detail carries fourteen veins per side; Compact carries six. These distinguish the selected form from the existing Wych elm drawing with its upper lateral apex points and forked upper veins. References: [Fragnière, Küffer & Kozlowski (2022), table 1](https://www.infoflora.ch/assets/content/documents/publication/Y_Fragniere_2022_Bestimmung_europaeischen_Ulmen.pdf), [Bavarian State Institute of Forestry](https://lwf.bayern.de/boden-klima/baumartenwahl/235450/index.php). Fine veins stop slightly inside the teeth to preserve the heavier stroke envelope; the icons do not reproduce every nerve-tooth junction.
- **Dog rose:** seven serrate leaflets and a pair of stipules attached along the petiole. Five-leaflet forms also occur. The terminal leaflet is distinct from the lateral pairs. Reference: [Flora of North America](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242417146). Compact preserves the stipules but reduces teeth and omits secondary venation. Fine hairs, glands and prickles are not represented.
- **Common hawthorn:** deep divisions and teeth concentrated toward the ends of five major lobes. Compact merges small teeth while retaining the incisions. Reference: [Oregon State University](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/crataegus-monogyna). Natural lobe counts vary.
- **European holly:** a selected spiny leaf, with a curved margin between spines. Adult foliage can also be less prickly or entire. Reference: [Oregon State University](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/ilex-aquifolium). The outline suggests margin undulation; it cannot reproduce its three-dimensional surface.
- **Montpellier maple:** a broad, three-lobed blade, entire margin, shallow cordate base and a long petiole. Reference: [Oregon State University](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/acer-monspessulanum), alongside the linked Info Flora description. Both sizes retain the three primary palmate veins.
- **Box elder:** one compound leaf with three leaflets; the terminal leaflet is lobed and has a longer petiolule than the lateral pair. Reference: [University of Texas, Vascular Plants of Williamson County](https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Acer%20negundo). The species also has forms with more leaflets. Leaflet serration is coarsened in Compact.
- **Bird cherry:** a selected rounded blade base, regular fine serration, acuminate apex and short petiole. Detail has ten vein pairs and two upper-petiole glands, within the ranges in [Flora of North America](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242341600). Compact reduces the veins and omits the glands. The longer petiole and unequal teeth of Wild cherry are retained in its separate drawing.

## Corrections made during review

The early drafts were revised before inclusion:

- Arcuate veins in Common dogwood and Cornelian cherry were redrawn as separate ascending arcs; none returns to or crosses the midrib a second time.
- Toothed flanks preserve apexward tooth orientation on both sides. Reversing a contour no longer reverses the tooth direction.
- Compound leaves were respaced, with species-specific leaflet widths and separate Compact adjustments. The painted outlines of neighbouring leaflets remain separated.
- The lower veins of European white elm were reshaped and their endpoints moved inward without pushing them down across the concave blade base.
- Holly, Wild service tree and Compact Guelder rose received local vein-end corrections where the first drafts left the blade.
- Montpellier maple's sinuses were made shallower after comparison, giving the three lobes a more continuous blade. Box elder retains a separate, longer terminal petiolule.

## Visual and technical checks

Both variants of every addition were inspected enlarged. Separate contact sheets checked 24/32 px Compact and 48/64 px Detail on light and dark backgrounds. Comparisons included the two limes, the two birches, the two elms, hornbeam, lobed leaves, compound leaves and the cherry species. The limes and several oval shrub leaves naturally overlap in outline; surface and shoot characters are deliberately documented as absent rather than replaced with artificial identifying marks.

All 104 SVGs undergo the geometry, paint, metadata and browser checks in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md). The four new artwork regression tests protect selected leaflet counts, stipules, petiolar glands and declared vein counts. These are drawing regressions, not a biological certification.

A byte comparison with the canonical v1.1.0 ZIP verified that all 56 earlier source SVGs are unchanged. The gallery, sprites, manifest and overview were regenerated from the current source files. The release builder verifies the final ZIP against the current tracked project files, including the newly added artwork.

The result is a source-guided icon collection. Independent botanical review, microscopic characters, natural population variation and complete identification from a leaf alone remain outside the evidence supplied by these checks.
