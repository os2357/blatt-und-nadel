# Full collection review — 1.8.0

13 October 2026 · **134 taxa / 268 source SVGs** · baseline: canonical v1.7.0 ZIP

All 126 existing primary catalog descriptions were retrieved and reread. Both variants of every existing taxon were inspected enlarged alongside foliage references, followed by target-size review at Compact 24/32 px and Detail 48/64 px on light and dark backgrounds. Eight additions passed through [two reviewed groups](EXPANSION-1.8.md), including a second enlarged photo comparison and direct comparison with similar species.

This is a fresh internal design review. It is not independent botanical certification or a measured specimen series. The primary references were retrieved anew; supplementary links retained from previous audits do not imply that every historical treatment was reread.

## Results and preservation

**All 252 previous SVGs are byte-identical to v1.7.0.** This includes the corrected Stieleiche, Ginkgo, Bergulme, Hainbuche, Schwarzpappel and all conifers. Sixteen new source SVGs bring the collection to 268. No previously fixed drawing has been regenerated or rolled back.

The [machine-readable audit](audit-1.8.json) records each SVG's current and baseline hash, role counts and blade bounds, alongside the selected form, fresh finding, reference URLs and omissions. “Retained” below means inspected and unchanged, not skipped.

## Findings in the new drawings

- Ohrweide and Grauweide: reduced excessive draft vein curvature; shortened Grauweide's initial apex. Both use regional leaf forms with explicit stipule reductions in Compact.
- Mandelweide and Lorbeerweide: checked stalk glands and selected regional blade proportions separately. Cultivated New Zealand forms were compared without treating their wider/narrower extremes as universal requirements.
- Zwergbirke: retained a rounded, broader-than-long blade and three vein pairs; the natural tiny size is not implied by the icon's display size.
- Rauschbeere: explicitly uses the narrower Central European taxonomic concept of _Vaccinium uliginosum_, rather than silently folding in _V. gaultherioides_.
- Netzweide: replaced a too-regular ornamental draft mesh with ascending veins and sparse irregular crosslinks. Both dwarf willows retain the two near-basal vein pairs described in FNA.

The validator also exposed a false positive: its case-insensitive global search for “NaN” rejected the label “Betula nana”. Nonfinite numeric sentinels are now rejected in path geometry; XML safety checks still cover the entire source. Malformed-path regressions include NaN, Infinity, undefined and overflow.

## Technical checks and limits

The release checks cover XML/metadata, accessible names, geometry, separated blades, closed contours, visible vein-origin attachment, full 16× painted-stroke containment, generated-file freshness and browser rendering. Exact release results and browser versions are in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md).

These monochrome drawings omit most hairs, texture, lower-surface colour, cross-sections and complete tertiary venation. Species, subspecies and hybrids may overlap in leaf shape. Stated counts describe the selected drawing unless explicitly tied to a flora. No remaining contradiction was identified for the selected forms in this review; this is a bounded review outcome, not a guarantee of universally exact anatomy or identification.
