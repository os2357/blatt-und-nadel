# Full collection review · v1.9.0

13 October 2026 · **142 taxa / 284 source SVGs** · baseline v1.8.0

Fresh internal source-guided review of all 142 taxa and both optical variants. All 134 existing primary descriptions and their reference photographs were retrieved again; each old drawing was inspected enlarged alongside foliage and at target sizes on light/dark backgrounds. Eight additions underwent two sequential research/drawing/review rounds and similar-species comparisons. Every previous SVG is byte-identical to the canonical v1.8.0 baseline. This is not independent botanical certification or a specimen-measured illustration series.

## Evidence and procedure

All 134 previous primary treatments and their photographic references were retrieved anew. The enlarged review used 34 four-taxon boards with both drawings and reference photos; the target-size review covered every existing SVG at Compact 24/32 px and Detail 48/64 px on light and dark backgrounds. The eight new taxa were researched and drawn in two groups, reviewed against photographs, then compared with neighboring species. New artwork received a repeated enlarged and target-size check after refinement.

The primary leaf descriptions were read individually, including the Acer palmatum treatment within the Flora of China Acer PDF. Ten other non-InfoFlora pages were read for their relevant leaf passages. Historical supplementary links are retained as provenance, without implying that every old bibliography link was opened again. For the additions, blocked eFloras pages were supplemented by the indexed treatment text and the other named sources.

Silhouette, proportions, maximum-width position, apex, base, relative stalk length, margin, principal veins, needle/fascicle insertion and leaflet origins were checked. Natural overlap was retained. The former suggestion to give Acer pseudoplatanus universally rounded sinuses was not adopted: the consulted description supports acute sinuses. Leaf-only separation of limes, willows, elder species and cultivated/wild fruit taxa remains limited.

## Corrections during drawing

- Pimpernuss: shortened initially protruding leaflet veins.
- Schwarznuss: revised leaflet spacing, orientation and narrow-leaflet stroke weights; retained fifteen leaflets and a small terminal leaflet.
- Blumenesche: separated crowded upper/middle leaflet contours, including the heavier Compact outlines; retained longer terminal petiolule.
- All 268 older SVGs remain byte-identical. No previously corrected oak, elm, pine or Ginkgo was regenerated.

## Reproducible checks

The [JSON audit](audit-1.9.json) contains one record per taxon, source and photo URLs, selected-form limits, per-variant hashes and contour bounds. The new audit validator binds this record to the exact files, catches missing optical variants and rejects stale release metadata or SVG hashes. It verifies evidence consistency, not botanical truth. See [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md) for the geometry/browser gates and [EXPANSION-1.9.md](EXPANSION-1.9.md) for the additions.

Bounds are axis-aligned drawing measurements, not physical specimen measurements or true longitudinal ratios of rotated leaflets. Photographs were consulted without tracing or redistribution. The collection remains a carefully referenced icon set, not an independently reviewed scientific atlas.
