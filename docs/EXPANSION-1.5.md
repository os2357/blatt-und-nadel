# Expansion review — 1.5.0

12 October 2026 · 84 → 100 species · 168 → 200 SVGs

The additions were developed in two groups of eight, with description, enlarged-render and geometry checks between groups. After the Ginkgo concern exposed the limits of the earlier review, **all 100 species received an additional photograph comparison**, including both new groups. This final pass led to further corrections; the first check was not treated as sufficient evidence of accuracy.

## Round one: shrubs and riparian foliage

| Species                             | Form selected and review focus                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Common box — _Buxus sempervirens_   | Entire oval blade, rounded apex, short petiole. Faint natural venation is emphasized; the detached leaf does not show opposite insertion.                    |
| Common lilac — _Syringa vulgaris_   | Broad cordate, entire blade with a prolonged apex and conspicuous petiole.                                                                                   |
| Mezereon — _Daphne mezereum_        | Oblanceolate blade, upper maximum width, long taper toward the short stalk. Compact lower veins removed to keep this taper open.                             |
| Purple willow — _Salix purpurea_    | Upper maximum width; serration limited to the upper two thirds. A low Detail vein was shortened to remain within the taper.                                  |
| Common osier — _Salix viminalis_    | Long narrow blade, lower maximum width and weakly wavy margin. Very narrow Compact strokes keep both sides visible.                                          |
| Oregon grape — _Mahonia aquifolium_ | Seven broad prickly leaflets. Photo comparison rejected excessively deep spines and narrow blades; upper leaflet clearance was subsequently corrected.       |
| European dewberry — _Rubus caesius_ | Three broad leaflets, stalked terminal leaflet and broad paired stipules in Detail. Lateral leaves remain nearly sessile; optical connectors are documented. |
| Blackcurrant — _Ribes nigrum_       | Broad three-lobed selected form with a cordate base. A geometric star-like draft was replaced after comparison with leaf photographs.                        |

Primary descriptions: Flora Helvetica through the species-specific InfoFlora pages linked in [BOTANY.md](BOTANY.md), with [FloraWeb for Common osier](https://www.floraweb.de/php/artenhome.php?suchnr=5206). Photographic provenance is recorded in [audit-1.5.json](audit-1.5.json).

Primary descriptions: the individual InfoFlora pages in [BOTANY.md](BOTANY.md), [Oregon State University for Bald cypress](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/taxodium-distichum), [Flora of China for needle shape](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=2&taxon_id=200005404), and the [Virginia Tech Black pine account](https://dendro.cnre.vt.edu/dendrology/syllabus/factsheet.cfm?ID=106). Botanical photographs were reviewed separately from prose descriptions. Photographs used only for comparison are not redistributed with the artwork.

## Review decisions

Both variants of every addition were inspected enlarged and at 24/32 px Compact and 48/64 px Detail on light and dark backgrounds. Compound-leaf gaps, narrow-blade interiors, teeth and terminal leaflet spacing were checked explicitly. The source is the editable SVG; no clipping masks conceal faulty geometry.

The drawings remain selected, reduced forms. Small-size needle counts, tooth counts and most secondary veins are optical choices. [BOTANY.md](BOTANY.md) gives the omissions for each species. The [complete audit](AUDIT-1.5.md) includes existing species and the six existing-species redraws.
