# Expansion review — 1.6.0

13 October 2026 · 100 → **117 species** · two consecutive groups of eight, then European plum

The first group fills heathland, broom and woody-climber gaps and extends the existing Ribes comparison. It was drawn and checked before choosing the second group of widely encountered park and garden trees/shrubs. Both groups preserve the 96 × 96, `currentColor`, separate Detail/Compact system. Existing artwork was preserved from the canonical v1.5 archive; only Ginkgo was subsequently redrawn after renewed feedback.

This completes the requested expansion round. Further additions should follow a concrete content gap, with another reference gate before drawing. Independent specialist review of the existing collection remains more valuable than an arbitrary larger species count.

## Targeted addition: European plum

After the two groups were complete, the user identified the missing Pflaume. Both variants were added as `pflaume` · _Prunus domestica_. The [full FNA treatment](https://floranorthamerica.org/Prunus_domestica) supports elliptic to obovate leaves, a cuneate to obtuse base, variable short-pointed apex and crenate-serrate teeth. [InfoFlora subsp. domestica](https://www.infoflora.ch/de/flora/prunus-domestica-subsp-domestica.html) describes sharper teeth than [subsp. insititia](https://www.infoflora.ch/de/flora/prunus-domestica-subsp-insititia.html). The species-level icon therefore selects one fine, blunt-toothed form and does not claim cultivar or subspecies identification.

Three [Bochum Botanical Association foliage photographs](https://botanik-bochum.de/web/pflanzenbilder/Prunus_domestica.htm) were inspected, including the more rounded insititia form. The selected drawing is elliptic-obovate, widest above mid-blade, with a tapering base, short acute tip and short petiole. Detail has eight drawn lateral vein pairs, Compact three; hairs, minute tooth glands and optional petiolar glands are omitted. Both variants were checked beside Blackthorn, Wild cherry and Peach, enlarged and at their target sizes on light/dark backgrounds. Natural overlap remains. Full contour and stroke-containment checks passed before regenerating the collection and rerunning the browser checks.
